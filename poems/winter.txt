The winter moon rests pale above the pine,
And snow erases footprints by the gate.
An icicle lets slip a bead of shine,
While frozen branches bow beneath their weight.
Inside, the kettle hums its little song,
A book lies open near the amber fire.
The shadows of the chairs grow thin and long,
And sparks climb briefly upward, then expire.
We watch the snowfall till the clock strikes late.
